Our Conferences

Be refreshed, equipped, and transformed through conferences designed to deepen your relationship with God, strengthen your faith, and empower you to make a lasting impact.

This is a weekend programme that enables people to live life in abundance and experience freedom from rejection, curses and other areas they are struggling with. The teachings help establish a Godly foundation and answer various questions that people have about God. Through these sessions, we have witnessed many people healed and restored.

This 7-day programme aids in bringing healing & wholeness to lives that have been damaged in spirit, soul, and body. The aim of this conference is to bring people an awareness of areas of captivity in their lives and establish a lifestyle of freedom. All of us undergo disappointment, hurt, and trauma; and if not dealt with, can often be held captive to them for life.

We aim to be a community which fosters collaboration between diverse segments of the Body of Christ, working towards building a healthy global Church. At the Global Pastors Conference we are committed to working with pastors and leaders, providing them with practical training to address the unique challenges of the 21st Century Church.
