Equipping the Next Generation of Leaders

“Their responsibility is to equip God’s people to do his work and build up the church, the body of Christ”
Ephesians 4:12 (NLT)

Adonai Bible School (ABS) is a church-based theological training program dedicated to equipping the people of God for works of service, for the building up of the church. The church is built up as believers grow into maturity and as more believers are added to its number. We are committed to providing foundational theological education that equips the next generation for church leadership, church growth, and church planting.

Our learning program places importance on a growing relationship of loving obedience to Jesus Christ. Each module is biblically grounded and integrates real-world ministry insights, with an emphasis on intellectual formation [Head], ministry formation [Hands], and spiritual formation [Heart] for a holistic and integrated learning experience.

ACADEMIC YEAR

The academic year runs from June through March, with a winter break in December. The year is structured into quarters, with each quarter consisting of six modules. The schedule is as follows: June to August (Q1), September to November (Q2), and January to March (Q3).

Enrollment Options

Certificate of Achievement in Ministry

This certificate is awarded based on full attendance at all 18 modules across the core disciplines of Theology, Old Testament, New Testament, Mission, and Leadership.

Graduate Diploma in Ministry

This Advanced Diploma is recognized as equivalent to the first year of a Master’s-level degree. Eligibility for the award is based upon full attendance across all 18 modules, along with the successful completion of all required assignments and module tests. Furthermore, candidates must fulfil practical ministry requirements in Spiritual Formation [KVL],Discipleship [BGF], and Pastoral Care & Counselling [WFC I & II].
